# Third-party media tooling

The V3 demonstration uses the stock `af_heart` synthetic voice from [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), whose model weights are distributed under Apache License 2.0. Local inference uses [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), distributed under the MIT License, copyright 2025 github.com/thewh1teagle.

Only generated narration is included in the demonstration. The model weights, voice pack, inference runtime and their dependencies are not included in the release. This is a stock synthetic voice; no person's voice was cloned for HardStop. See the upstream [model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) and [runtime license](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/blob/main/LICENSE) for their terms and attribution information.
